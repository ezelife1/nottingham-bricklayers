How your enquiry works

1. Describe the job

Share your name, contact number, postcode and the work you need. Approximate measurements and photographs help establish the scope.

2. Discuss availability and a quote

Your details may be shared with a suitable independent contractor to discuss your request. Coverage, response time and a match are not guaranteed.

3. Choose your contractor

Check insurance, experience and references. Agree the scope, payment terms and any guarantees directly with the contractor before work starts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I request a bricklaying quote?

Send your postcode, a short description, approximate measurements and photographs if available. We use your enquiry to seek a suitable local contractor, subject to coverage and availability. A visit may be needed before a price can be confirmed.

How much does brickwork cost in Nottingham?

The price depends on dimensions, foundations, access, brick matching, scaffolding and waste removal. Ask the contractor for a written quotation that states what is included and whether VAT applies.

Can I enquire about a small repair?

Yes. Describe the damage and its location. Small brickwork repairs and repointing enquiries are welcome, although contractor availability and minimum job charges can vary.

Who carries out and guarantees the work?

Nottingham Bricklayers is an enquiry service. An independent contractor provides the quotation and carries out any agreed work. Check their identity, insurance, relevant experience, references and any written guarantee before appointing them.

Which Nottingham areas can I enquire from?

Enquiries are welcome from Nottingham and nearby areas including Beeston, Arnold, Carlton, Mapperley, Sherwood, Wollaton and West Bridgford. Coverage must be confirmed for your postcode; a contractor match is not guaranteed.
