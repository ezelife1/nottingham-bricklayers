Enquiry service terms

Nottingham Bricklayers handles requests for contact about brickwork. It does not provide building work, structural advice or a guarantee of contractor availability.

Independent quotations and contracts

Any site assessment, quotation, building contract, payment arrangement or workmanship guarantee is agreed directly between you and your chosen contractor. An enquiry does not oblige you to appoint anyone.

Check the contractor

Confirm the contractor’s identity, insurance, relevant experience, scope of work, price, payment schedule and any necessary permissions before work starts. Information on this website is general guidance and cannot replace a site assessment.

Website information

Images supplied with this site are illustrative and their project provenance is unverified. No business history, ratings or completed-project claims are made by this enquiry service.

Questions

Use the contact page for questions about the enquiry service. These terms do not affect statutory consumer rights.
